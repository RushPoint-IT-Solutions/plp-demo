# PLP All-Modules Consolidated Process Flow Guide

This guide accompanies the editable one-page Visio swimlane showing how every documented PLP module participates in one controlled academic lifecycle.

| Phase | Modules and activities | Exit validation gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. GOVERN | 10 System Configuration; 11 Access Control; 35 Data Administration; 00 System Overview; 01 Access and Login; 02 Password Lifecycle | G1 - Authorized configuration, identity, access, and controlled source data |
| 2. BUILD FOUNDATION | 12 Academic Master Data; 13 Curriculum Workflow; 16 Resource Setup; 23 Grading Configuration; Curriculum review; and approval; Dependency, integrity,; and publication validation | G2 - Published curriculum, valid resources, and approved grading rules |

SUPPORT & OUTPUT | 27 Scholarships; 28 Student Welfare; 30 Official Documents; 32 Reports and Graduation; 31 Student Requests; 33 Faculty Evaluation; View approved grades,; documents, and status; Eligibility, finalized source data,; and report/document controls | G7 - Finalized source data, eligibility, clearance, and document controls |
